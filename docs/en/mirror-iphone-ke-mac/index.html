Your iPhone on your Mac, three ways.

Apple offers several routes to get an iPhone screen onto a Mac, each with its own limits. Djie Cast puts them on one page so you can pick the one that fits.

Download for MacSee all features

Step by step

  1. Plug in a USB cable

    Connect the iPhone to the Mac with a data cable and unlock it.

  2. Tap “Trust”

    When the iPhone asks “Trust This Computer?”, tap Trust and enter your passcode.

  3. Allow Camera for Djie Cast

    macOS reads the iPhone screen as a video source, so allow Camera access when asked.

  4. Press Start Cast

    The iPhone's screen and audio appear in a Mac window that turns when the iPhone does.

Want to control the iPhone from the Mac?

Apple only allows control through iPhone Mirroring (macOS 15 and iOS 18 or later, same Apple ID). Djie Cast opens it from the iPhone page.

iPhone camera and gallery

With Continuity Camera, the iPhone's back camera shows on the Mac without a cable. Over the cable you can browse and download the photo and video gallery, with battery and storage info.

Questions

Do I need to install an app on the iPhone?
No. Every route uses built-in iOS and macOS features.
Why doesn't my iPhone show up?
Check that the cable supports data, the iPhone is unlocked, and you tapped Trust.
Does it work over Wi-Fi?
Yes, with AirPlay (view only) or iPhone Mirroring (full control).
