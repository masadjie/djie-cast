scrcpy on Mac, no terminal.

scrcpy is the best way to show and control an Android phone from a computer, but on a Mac it usually means Homebrew and the terminal. Djie Cast ships scrcpy and adb inside the app, so you plug in a cable and press one button.

Download for MacSee all features

Step by step

  1. Download Djie Cast

    Get the latest DMG from the GitHub releases page and drag Djie Cast to Applications. scrcpy and adb are already inside.

  2. Turn on USB Debugging

    On the phone: Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times, then open Developer options and turn on USB Debugging.

  3. Plug in and allow

    Connect the phone to the Mac and tap Allow on the “Allow USB debugging?” prompt.

  4. Press Start Cast

    The phone appears as a card on the Android page. One click and its window opens, ready for mouse and keyboard.

Every scrcpy option, no flags to remember

Quality, bitrate, fps, audio, screen off, stay awake, show touches, always on top, fullscreen and recording are switches and buttons. Power users can still add their own arguments.

More than mirroring

Device cards show the phone's specs, battery health and cycles, and free storage. There's also an Android file manager, an app list with uninstall, and a phone-camera mode.

Questions

Do I need to install scrcpy with Homebrew?
No. Djie Cast bundles the official scrcpy and adb.
Does it work over Wi-Fi?
Yes. Phones with Wireless debugging on the same network show up automatically; pair once with the 6-digit code.
Is Djie Cast free?
Yes, free and open source under the MIT license.
