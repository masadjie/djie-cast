Mirror your Android phone to your Mac.

With Djie Cast your Android screen appears on your Mac in seconds and you control it with the Mac's mouse and keyboard. Handy for presentations, app demos, live streaming, or just answering chats without picking up the phone.

Download for MacSee all features

Step by step

  1. Install Djie Cast

    Download the DMG and drag it to Applications. First launch: right-click → Open.

  2. Turn on USB Debugging

    Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times → Developer options → USB Debugging. On Xiaomi also enable “USB debugging (Security settings)”.

  3. Plug in the cable

    Use a data cable and tap Allow on the phone. Tick “Always allow from this computer”.

  4. Start casting

    Open the Android page and press Start Cast. For Wi-Fi, turn on Wireless debugging and pick the phone under Phones on this network.

Your Mac keyboard becomes a remote

⌘B back, ⌘H home, ⌘S recent apps, ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ volume, ⌘R rotate, ⌘V paste text from the Mac to the phone. Right-click also works as back.

For OBS and live streaming

OBS, Streamlabs or Ecamm Live can capture the cast window with Window Capture. Turn on Borderless window for a clean picture.

Questions

My phone isn't detected — why?
Usually USB Debugging is off, the cable is charge-only, or the permission prompt on the phone wasn't tapped. Djie Cast has a step-by-step guide on the Android page.
Is the audio forwarded?
Yes, on Android 11 and later the phone's audio plays on the Mac.
Does the phone need root?
No root and nothing to install on the phone.
